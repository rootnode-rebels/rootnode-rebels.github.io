★ Neighborhood Delivery Routine

🚲 The Bread Bike: Our delivery rider pedals freshly baked baguettes and country sourdough boxes directly to our neighboring street doors twice daily: at 8:00 AM for morning breakfast and 4:30 PM for dinner hearth tables.

🥐 Walk-in Bread Table: Loaves come off the stone hearth every morning at 7:30 AM. When the chalkboard says "SOLD OUT," that means we’re already proofing tomorrow's wild yeast dough!

🥖 Chat with the bakers: "Planning a breakfast gathering, office celebration, or Sunday family brunch? Tell us a couple days ahead and we'll pack a big warm wooden crate with fresh sourdough, warm cardamom knots, and jugs of slow-drip coffee."
